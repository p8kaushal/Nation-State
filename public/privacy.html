Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Nation State is a strategy game. This policy explains the information the game uses when you play and when you choose to sign in.

Information used by the game

You can play without an account. The game stores your progress, settings, achievements and run history in your browser's local storage. This data stays in that browser unless you choose to use account features. Clearing your browser's site data can remove it.

If you sign in with Google, Google provides account information such as your name, email address and profile picture. Supabase handles authentication and stores the account profile. When signed in, the game can also store your saved games, completed runs and achievements in Supabase so they can be accessed across devices.

How information is used

Account information is used to identify you, display your profile and connect you to your own game data. Game data is used to save progress, show your history and achievements, and provide the game. The game runs on Vercel, which may process technical request information as part of hosting and security.

Google, Supabase and Vercel process information to provide their respective services. Their own privacy policies describe their practices: Google, Supabase and Vercel.

Storage and deletion

Cloud account and game data are kept until deleted or until you request account deletion. You can delete the current scenario's save and clear past runs from within the game. These controls do not delete your account or every achievement. To request access to or deletion of your account and associated cloud data, open a support request. GitHub issues are public: do not include your email address or other private information. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting on a request.

Local browser data is managed separately: clear this site's data in your browser to remove local saves, history and achievements. Copies in backups, if any, may remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, open a support request. GitHub issues are public, so do not include personal information. This policy may be updated as the game changes; the date above identifies the latest version.
